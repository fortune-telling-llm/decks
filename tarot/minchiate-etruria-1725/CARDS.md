# Minchiate Etruria (Florence, 1725) — Cards

This file defines the historical **97-card** sequence documented for Minchiate Etruria (Florence, 1725).

The table uses stable repository sequence numbers while preserving historical card numbers, titles, ranks, and edition-specific exceptions inside the card name or description. Short descriptions identify the card’s primary visual or structural role; they are not substitutes for future image-led card files.

Minchiate contains the Fool, forty numbered trumps, and the usual 56 suit cards. Its expanded sequence adds theological and cardinal virtues, four elements, and twelve zodiac signs. It omits several familiar tarot subjects, including the Popess, Pope, and Empress as separate standard trumps.
