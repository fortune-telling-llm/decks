# Jacques Viéville Tarot (Paris, c. 1650) — Cards

This file defines the historical **78-card** sequence documented for Jacques Viéville Tarot (Paris, c. 1650).

The table uses stable repository sequence numbers while preserving historical card numbers, titles, ranks, and edition-specific exceptions inside the card name or description. Short descriptions identify the card’s primary visual or structural role; they are not substitutes for future image-led card files.

Repository titles are normalized for navigation. The original pack’s trump captions, order, and imagery diverge from the later Marseille standard: Justice is VII, the Chariot VIII, Strength IX, the Old Man or Hermit XI, and trump XVI is a lightning-struck tree rather than the usual tower.
