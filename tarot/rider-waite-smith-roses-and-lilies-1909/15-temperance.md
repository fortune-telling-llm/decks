# 15 — XIV — Temperance

## Card metadata

- **Deck:** Rider–Waite–Smith Tarot — Original Roses & Lilies Edition (1909)
- **Arcana:** Major Arcana
- **Repository sequence number:** 15
- **Historical card number:** XIV
- **Printed title:** `TEMPERANCE.`
- **Primary polarity:** integrating, moderating, healing, circulating
- **Primary esoteric correspondence:** Sagittarius and the Hebrew letter Samekh in the Golden Dawn/Waite–Smith system; neither attribution is printed on the card
- **Core theme:** different substances, needs, and rhythms become workable through continuous exchange, measured adjustment, and a path that connects embodied reality with a larger aim
- **Artwork status:** this description targets Pamela Colman Smith’s first 1909 Roses & Lilies design. Any uploaded image must be checked against an early-edition scan for exact colors, flowers, vessel positions, inscriptions, linework, cropping, restoration, and print wear.

## Literal visual description

### Overall composition

The card is vertically oriented with the Roman numeral **`XIV`** at the top and **`TEMPERANCE.`** in the lower title panel. A large winged angel stands at the edge of a pool. One foot rests on land and the other stands in water. The angel pours liquid between two cups held at different heights.

A white robe covers the body. On the chest is a white square containing a triangle. A small solar or circular sign appears on the forehead. Yellow flowers grow beside the water. A narrow path extends from the pool through green terrain toward two mountains, between which a bright crown-like light or radiant opening appears.

The composition links the immediate act of pouring with a distant destination: **forehead light → cups and flowing liquid → triangle and square → feet on water and land → path → crowned horizon**.

### The angel

The central figure faces the viewer while concentrating on the cups.

- Large red wings extend behind the body.
- The expression is calm and attentive.
- The stance is open and balanced.
- The figure occupies two environments at once.
- The body is neither rigid nor unstable.

The angel can symbolize a mediating intelligence, healer, coordinator, messenger, or the capacity within a person or system to translate between different states without erasing either.

### The two cups

The angel holds one cup higher and one lower.

- Liquid visibly travels between them.
- The stream appears controlled and continuous.
- The vessels remain distinct.
- The exchange is active rather than completed.

The cups can symbolize emotion, resources, substances, perspectives, people, systems, or stages of a process. Temperance does not simply average them into sameness; it tests ratio, flow, and mutual effect.

### The flowing liquid

The liquid moves in an unusual diagonal stream.

- It appears to pass cleanly from one vessel to the other.
- It does not spill visibly onto the ground.
- Its direction can become visually ambiguous depending on how the card is viewed.

The stream can signify communication, dosage, feedback, compromise, translation, emotional regulation, chemistry, or iterative adjustment. The direction ambiguity supports reciprocity: influence may move both ways.

### One foot on land and one in water

The angel stands across a boundary.

- One foot is grounded on earth.
- The other is immersed in the pool.
- Neither element is abandoned.

Land can represent material reality, body, facts, routine, and structure. Water can represent emotion, intuition, memory, and fluid response. The stance demonstrates integration through simultaneous contact, not escape from one side.

### The triangle within the square

A triangle is displayed inside a square on the chest.

- The square creates a containing frame.
- The triangle points upward.
- The emblem sits near the heart and center of the body.

The square can symbolize material form, fourfold structure, or embodiment. The triangle can symbolize aspiration, synthesis, spirit, or the third form created by two interacting forces. Their combination suggests a higher principle working inside practical containment.

### The forehead symbol

A small circular or solar mark appears on the angel’s forehead.

- It is centered above the eyes.
- It links perception with illumination.
- It is subtler than the large sun in other cards.

The mark can signify conscious purpose, attention, clarity, or the ability to perceive proportion rather than react from one extreme.

### The yellow flowers

Yellow flowers, commonly read as irises, grow near the water.

- They are rooted at the boundary between land and pool.
- Their color echoes the distant light.
- Their upright forms add living evidence of successful integration.

Flowers can signify healing, communication, cultivation, and beauty produced where different resources meet.

### The path, mountains, and crown-like light

A narrow path begins near the water and winds toward two mountains.

- The path is visible but not straight.
- It passes through green land.
- Between the mountains glows a bright crown-like or radiant form.

The path shows that moderation is not a static midpoint. It is a sustained practice leading somewhere. The distant crown can symbolize purpose, attainment, healing, vocation, or a larger integration not reached through extremes.

### Color relationships and visual movement

The dominant relationships are:

- **white robe:** clarity, spaciousness, and a neutral vessel;
- **red wings:** vitality, active mediation, and transformative energy;
- **blue water:** emotion, healing, and circulation;
- **green land:** embodied life, routine, and growth;
- **yellow flowers and horizon:** illumination, purpose, and visible result;
- **square and triangle:** containment and synthesis.

The eye repeatedly moves between pairs—cup to cup, water to land, mountain to mountain—while the centered angel holds the exchange.

## Core meaning

Temperance represents **healing and workable integration through proportion, circulation, and repeated adjustment**.

It can signify moderation, compatibility, blending, recovery, patience, mediation, coordination, adaptation, sustainable routine, chemistry, translation, and the creation of a third solution from previously separate elements.

The card does not demand bland compromise or suppression of difference. The cups remain distinct, and one foot remains in each element. Healthy Temperance finds a ratio that preserves what matters while allowing exchange.

Its shadow includes dilution of essential needs, chronic self-suppression, incompatible elements forced together, poor dosage, overaccommodation, avoidance of necessary conflict, or maintaining apparent calm while one side carries the entire adjustment.

Waite’s published divinatory range includes economy, moderation, frugality, management, and accommodation; his reversed range includes religion, priesthood, disunion, unfortunate combinations, and competing interests. These meanings preserve the card’s practical concern with management and the risk of failed mixture.

## Symbol-by-symbol interpretation

### `XIV` — transformation brought into workable form

The number `14` follows Death’s `13`, suggesting the practical integration required after an ending. Reduced to `5`, it carries movement, change, and testing.

Repeated fourteens or fives may highlight adaptation, friction, healing, or the need to keep adjusting rather than seek a perfect fixed state.

### The two cups — distinct vessels in relationship

The cups may represent:

- two people;
- two departments;
- emotion and reason;
- work and rest;
- medicine and body;
- tradition and innovation;
- or any pair whose interaction must be measured.

The vessels do not disappear into one container. Compatibility includes boundaries.

### The stream — feedback and circulation

The liquid can symbolize:

- conversation;
- emotional exchange;
- resource allocation;
- chemical or practical dosage;
- creative revision;
- or the feedback loop through which balance is maintained.

### Land and water — embodiment and feeling

The two-foot stance asks whether a plan works both emotionally and materially. A solution that exists only in theory or only in feeling is incomplete.

### The triangle in square — spirit within structure

The emblem may indicate:

- purpose embodied in routine;
- insight contained in method;
- or a higher aim made practical through boundaries and form.

### The flowers — life at the interface

The flowers show that productive growth can occur at a boundary. They may point to healing, art, communication, or a relationship that flourishes because differences are managed rather than denied.

### The path — moderation as movement

The path makes Temperance directional. The question is not only “How do I calm this?” but “What larger destination does this balanced practice serve?”

### The crown-like horizon — a goal beyond immediate relief

The distant light can symbolize mastery, vocation, health, integration, or a result that becomes possible through sustained proportion rather than dramatic force.

## Divinatory interpretation by question type

### Love and relationships

Possible upright expressions include:

- compatibility built over time;
- healing after conflict;
- patient communication;
- blending different lifestyles or cultures;
- compromise that preserves both people;
- emotional regulation;
- or a relationship finding a sustainable rhythm.

In shadow it can indicate one person endlessly adapting, incompatible needs being minimized, conflict avoidance, or a bond maintained through dilution rather than genuine agreement.

### When asking about a specific person

The person may be:

- patient;
- diplomatic;
- healing-oriented;
- adaptable;
- measured;
- a mediator, coordinator, clinician, counselor, translator, chemist, artist, or integrator;
- or someone recovering balance after extremes.

Shadow possibilities include indecision, overaccommodation, blandness, hidden dysregulation, or difficulty taking a necessary firm position.

### The relationship between two people

The bond may be capable of integrating difference through:

- communication;
- pacing;
- shared routine;
- cultural translation;
- practical compromise;
- or mutual healing.

The card asks whether exchange flows both ways and whether both “cups” remain intact.

### Career, projects, and study

Temperance strongly favors:

- integration work;
- cross-functional coordination;
- migration;
- gradual rollout;
- quality tuning;
- mediation;
- sustainable scheduling;
- interdisciplinary study;
- and combining systems without losing essential functions.

It warns against rushed mergers, unclear interfaces, and compromise that removes the project’s core value.

### Money and material questions

The card can indicate budgeting, measured spending, diversification, gradual recovery, resource balancing, debt management, or combining income streams carefully.

In shadow it may show chronic shortfall, financial overextension, one account subsidizing another indefinitely, or a plan dependent on perfect conditions.

### Creativity

Temperance supports mixed media, editing, collaboration, color balance, iterative refinement, and combining influences into a distinct third form.

Its shadow is work so moderated that it loses energy or identity.

### Spirituality and personal growth

The card can symbolize embodied practice, integration of spiritual and ordinary life, healing, patience, and a middle path that remains dynamic.

It warns against bypassing anger, grief, sexuality, or conflict in the name of serenity.

## Intentions

As intentions, Temperance commonly indicates a desire to:

- reconcile;
- heal;
- negotiate;
- proceed gradually;
- find compatibility;
- blend resources;
- reduce extremes;
- or build a sustainable arrangement.

The intention is usually constructive but may be too cautious if a decisive boundary is required.

As feelings, it can show:

- calm affection;
- patience;
- willingness to understand;
- emotional recovery;
- cautious hope;
- desire for peace;
- or a growing sense that differences can be managed.

## Actions / likely next move

Likely actions include:

- reopening dialogue;
- adjusting pace;
- making a compromise;
- coordinating schedules or resources;
- beginning a healing routine;
- testing a new mixture;
- or continuing gradual integration.

In shadow, action may remain endlessly provisional or avoid the conflict that must be named.

## Advice

Temperance advises:

> **Keep both feet in reality, preserve the integrity of each vessel, and adjust the flow repeatedly until the relationship, routine, or system can sustain life over time.**

Do not call self-erasure compromise.

## Outcome

As an outcome, the card often indicates:

- gradual healing;
- workable compatibility;
- successful integration;
- moderation;
- a sustainable routine;
- or progress along a longer path.

The result is usually stable because it is tuned rather than forced, but it may take time.

## Yes / no

**Leaning: Yes through patience, integration, moderation, and repeated adjustment; conditional where the elements are fundamentally incompatible or one side refuses reciprocal change.**

## Timing

Temperance can indicate:

- gradual development;
- after several rounds of adjustment;
- during recovery;
- a fourteen-unit period when numerology is intentionally used;
- or a Sagittarius-linked period when that overlay is deliberately applied.

It is rarely immediate.

## Health / wellbeing readings

Tarot is not a medical diagnosis. In a non-diagnostic wellbeing context, Temperance may symbolize dosage, hydration, moderation, rehabilitation, coordinated care, sleep and activity balance, substance recovery, or gradual nervous-system regulation.

Medication, substance use, nutrition, and treatment changes require qualified professional guidance. The card must not be used to alter dosage independently.

## Image-led reading examples

### Example: “Can these two very different people make a relationship work?”

The two cups and two environments support difference, but only through reciprocal flow. Compatibility is possible if each person retains identity, both adjust, and the path serves a shared destination.

### Example: “How should we migrate this system?”

Use staged transfer, measurable interfaces, rollback capability, and continuous feedback. The stream should remain controlled; do not pour everything at once or lose either vessel’s essential function.

### Example: “What does recovery require?”

One foot in feeling and one in material routine: emotional support, qualified care, dosage or pacing, sleep, food, movement, and a long path toward a meaningful horizon. Avoid dramatic all-or-nothing changes.

## Historical and fortune-teller cautions

- Do not reduce Temperance to passive calm or bland compromise.
- Do not advise someone to tolerate incompatibility, abuse, or unequal labor for the sake of harmony.
- Do not use the card to recommend medication or substance dosage changes.
- Preserve the two cups, diagonal stream, land-water stance, triangle in square, flowers, path, mountains, and crown-like horizon as visible details.
- Keep Sagittarius/Samekh correspondence identified as an overlay, not printed evidence.
- Verify flower type, liquid direction, symbols, and colors against an actual 1909 Roses & Lilies scan.

## One-sentence essence

**Healing is an active circulation: keep each vessel whole, adjust the exchange with care, and let a sustainable rhythm carry difference toward a shared horizon.**
