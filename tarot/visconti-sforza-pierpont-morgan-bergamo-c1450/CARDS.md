# Visconti–Sforza Pierpont Morgan–Bergamo Tarocchi (c. 1450–1480) — Cards

This file defines the historical **78-card** sequence documented for Visconti–Sforza Pierpont Morgan–Bergamo Tarocchi (c. 1450–1480).

The table uses stable repository sequence numbers while preserving historical card numbers, titles, ranks, and edition-specific exceptions inside the card name or description. Short descriptions identify the card’s primary visual or structural role; they are not substitutes for future image-led card files.

The table records the conventional complete 78-card structure for reference, while explicitly marking the four original cards not present among the 74 surviving cards: the Devil, Tower, Three of Swords, and Knight of Coins. The surviving trumps were originally unnumbered and untitled; repository names are normalized scholarly identifications.
