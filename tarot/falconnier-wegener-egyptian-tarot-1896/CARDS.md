# Falconnier–Wegener Egyptian Tarot — Les XXII Lames Hermétiques (1896) — Cards

This file defines the historical **22-card** sequence documented for Falconnier–Wegener Egyptian Tarot — Les XXII Lames Hermétiques (1896).

The table uses stable repository sequence numbers while preserving historical card numbers, titles, ranks, and edition-specific exceptions inside the card name or description. Short descriptions identify the card’s primary visual or structural role; they are not substitutes for future image-led card files.

The 22 black-and-white plates are numbered `I–XXII`, with the Crocodile at `XXII` in this published sequence. Historical French titles are preserved with English navigation equivalents; this is not the later 78-card Saint-Germain expansion.

| Number | Card name | Short description |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **I — L’Initié / The Initiate** | A magician-like initiate represents conscious will, occult training, operation, and entry into the hermetic sequence. |
| 2 | **II — Le Sanctuaire / The Sanctuary** | A veiled sacred interior represents guarded knowledge, initiation, silence, polarity, and access to mystery. |
| 3 | **III — Isis** | The Egyptianized Empress figure represents generative nature, fertility, cosmic motherhood, and manifesting intelligence. |
| 4 | **IV — Le Pharaon / The Pharaoh** | Sovereign authority, order, law, worldly command, and the historical imagination of Egyptian kingship. |
| 5 | **V — Le Maître des Arcanes / Master of the Arcana** | Initiatory teacher, doctrine, transmission, sacred office, and control of esoteric knowledge. |
| 6 | **VI — Les Deux Routes / The Two Roads** | A divided path replaces the conventional Lovers title; desire, moral choice, influence, and consequential direction. |
| 7 | **VII — Le Char d’Osiris / Chariot of Osiris** | Triumphal movement, directed force, sovereignty, initiation, and mastery of opposing powers. |
| 8 | **VIII — La Balance et le Glaive / Balance and Sword** | Justice as equilibrium and cutting judgement; law, proportion, truth, and consequence. |
| 9 | **IX — La Lampe Voilée / The Veiled Lamp** | Hidden light, inward study, prudence, initiation, and knowledge protected from premature exposure. |
| 10 | **X — La Roue des Destinées / Wheel of Destinies** | Fate, cyclic change, rise and fall, cosmic mechanism, and turning conditions. |
| 11 | **XI — La Force / Strength** | Vital mastery, courage, command of instinct, and the disciplined application of force. |
| 12 | **XII — Le Pendu / The Hanged Man** | Suspension, sacrifice, reversal, initiation through suffering, and altered perspective. |
| 13 | **XIII — La Mort / Death** | Dissolution, mortality, ending, transformation, and passage into another condition. |
| 14 | **XIV — Les Deux Urnes / The Two Urns** | Temperance through paired vessels; mixture, circulation, balance, and controlled exchange. |
| 15 | **XV — Thyphon / Typhon** | An Egyptianized Devil analogue; chaos, destructive force, bondage, appetite, and unintegrated power. |
| 16 | **XVI — La Tour Foudroyée / The Lightning-Struck Tower** | Catastrophe, revelation, collapse of false structure, and violent release. |
| 17 | **XVII — L’Étoile / The Star** | Celestial guidance, hope, destiny, inspiration, and receptive openness. |
| 18 | **XVIII — La Lune / The Moon** | Reflection, generation, uncertainty, dream, hidden influence, and changing cycles. |
| 19 | **XIX — Le Soleil / The Sun** | Illumination, vitality, consciousness, success, union, and visible force. |
| 20 | **XX — Le Livre Sacré / The Sacred Book** | Falconnier’s title diverges from Judgement; revealed doctrine, record, awakening through knowledge, and authoritative text. |
| 21 | **XXI — La Couronne des Mages / Crown of the Magi** | World-like completion, mastery, initiation fulfilled, cosmic synthesis, and the crown of knowledge. |
| 22 | **XXII — Le Crocodile / The Crocodile** | The Fool analogue closes the published numbering; instinct, wandering, danger, innocence, and unbounded potential. |
