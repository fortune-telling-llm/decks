# Sola Busca Tarot (1491) — Cards

This file defines the historical **78-card** sequence documented for Sola Busca Tarot (1491).

The table uses stable repository sequence numbers while preserving historical card numbers, titles, ranks, and edition-specific exceptions inside the card name or description. Short descriptions identify the card’s primary visual or structural role; they are not substitutes for future image-led card files.

The 22 trumps are a unique sequence of named classical, biblical, and legendary figures rather than the later standard tarot subjects. All 56 suit cards are illustrated; their scenes must be read from the Sola Busca engravings and must not be replaced by Rider–Waite–Smith narratives.
