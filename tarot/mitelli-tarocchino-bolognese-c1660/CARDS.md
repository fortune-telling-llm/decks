# Giuseppe Maria Mitelli Tarocchino Bolognese (c. 1660–1665) — Cards

This file defines the historical **62-card** sequence documented for Giuseppe Maria Mitelli Tarocchino Bolognese (c. 1660–1665).

The table uses stable repository sequence numbers while preserving historical card numbers, titles, ranks, and edition-specific exceptions inside the card name or description. Short descriptions identify the card’s primary visual or structural role; they are not substitutes for future image-led card files.

Tarocchino shortens every suit by removing the Two, Three, Four, and Five. The pack therefore contains 22 trump cards and four ten-card suits: Ace, Six through Ten, Page, Knight, Queen, and King.
