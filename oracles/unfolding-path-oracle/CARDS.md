# The Unfolding Path Oracle — Cards

This file defines the planned **60-card** sequence for The Unfolding Path Oracle.

Each card describes a stage, condition, obstacle, acceleration, turning point, result, or redirection that can apply to relationships, work, projects, healing, decisions, travel, spiritual development, and other unfolding situations.

The cards describe the strongest present trajectory rather than an unchangeable fate. A delay may protect or prepare; a breakthrough may still require maintenance; a completion may become the first step of another cycle.

These short descriptions are planning summaries, not substitutes for future image-led card files. No individual card artwork or card-specific `.md` files are included yet.
