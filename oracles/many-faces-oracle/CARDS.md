# The Many Faces Oracle — Cards

This file defines the planned **72-card** sequence for The Many Faces Oracle.

Each card may identify a literal person or role, the context through which people know one another, a power relationship, a temporary part someone is playing, or an archetypal function within the situation. The same person may be represented by different cards in different questions or stages of life.

Roles do not determine character, compatibility, attraction, trustworthiness, or availability. Professional and caregiving roles may carry ethical boundaries that remain valid regardless of emotional intensity.

These short descriptions are planning summaries, not substitutes for future image-led card files. No individual artwork or card-specific `.md` files are included yet.

| Number | Card name | Short description |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **The Stranger** | Someone not yet meaningfully known, a chance encounter, anonymous influence, or a part of the future still without a face. |
| 2 | **The Familiar Face** | A person repeatedly seen or emotionally recognized before genuine closeness, identity, or compatibility is established. |
