# Beneath the Surface Oracle — Cards

This file defines the planned **60-card** sequence for Beneath the Surface Oracle.

Each card explores a plausible emotional, protective, practical, relational, or interpretive layer beneath a visible situation. The deck includes cards for projection, ordinary constraints, rightful privacy, accurate appearances, and genuine uncertainty so that “hidden” is never treated as automatically dramatic.

A card can suggest what to examine or ask about, but it is not proof of another person's private thoughts, secret actions, diagnosis, or intention. Direct communication and verified evidence remain primary.

These short descriptions are planning summaries, not substitutes for future image-led card files. No individual artwork or card-specific `.md` files are included yet.
