# The Web of Influence Oracle — Cards

This file defines the planned **60-card** sequence for The Web of Influence Oracle.

Each card is intentionally multivalent. Depending on the question, spread position, surrounding cards, and eventual artwork, the same card may describe:

- the material, symbolic, emotional, social, or practical condition currently present;
- the likely movement, obstacle, resource, warning, or opportunity;
- the role of timing, context, agency, evidence, and outside influence;
- the useful question, action, restraint, or boundary invited by the card;
- or the difference between a meaningful pattern and a conclusion that has not yet been established.

These short descriptions are planning summaries, not substitutes for future image-led card files. Artwork and detailed individual card Markdown files will be developed separately.

| Number | Card name | Short description |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **The Inner Circle** | A trusted core, leadership group, family unit, or close alliance holds privileged access and influence. |
| 2 | **The Outer Circle** | Peripheral membership, observation, limited access, emerging belonging, or distance from decision-making shapes the situation. |
| 3 | **The Outsider** | A person stands beyond the group's norms or access and may bring perspective, vulnerability, challenge, or freedom. |
| 4 | **The New Member** | A newcomer changes attention, roles, alliances, and the group's sense of itself. |
| 5 | **The Departing Member** | Someone is leaving a family, team, friendship group, organization, or shared identity, requiring redistribution and adjustment. |
| 6 | **The Gatekeeper** | One person or system controls access, introduction, approval, information, or legitimacy. |
| 7 | **The Sponsor** | A person with influence opens doors, offers protection, recommends, funds, or publicly supports another. |
| 8 | **The Mentor's Circle** | Guidance is shaped by a wider professional, spiritual, educational, or creative network. |
| 9 | **The Family Expectation** | Inherited roles, loyalty, culture, reputation, or obligation influences a personal choice. |
| 10 | **The Chosen Family** | Belonging is built through mutual recognition and care rather than blood or formal structure alone. |
| 11 | **The Family Secret** | Protected or concealed history shapes current roles, trust, and interpretation even when not openly discussed. |
| 12 | **The Family Council** | Several relatives or household members participate in a decision, conflict, care plan, or transition. |

| 37 | **The Witness Network** | Several people hold pieces of evidence, memory, validation, or accountability. |

| 58 | **The Group Dissolves** | A team, family structure, community, or alliance completes, fragments, or can no longer function. |
| 59 | **The New Network** | Fresh connections, institutions, friendships, or communities create different opportunities and expectations. |
| 60 | **The Web Reweaves** | After conflict, departure, truth, or change, influence redistributes and the collective forms a new pattern. |
